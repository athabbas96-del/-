## 2. Print specification

- 3mm bleed on every side for anything printed to the edge; keep text 4–5mm
  inside the trim.
- Artwork in CMYK (or spot colours) at 300 dpi at final size; logos and text as vectors.
- Export print-ready PDF (PDF/X-4 or as the printer specifies) with crop marks.
- Text below 7pt and fine serifs reversed out of dark colours tend to fill in;
  test at size.
- Specify paper (weight in gsm, coated/uncoated, colour) and finishes (foil,
  emboss, deboss, letterpress, edge colour) on the job sheet. Finishes are
  part of the identity: Velline uses blind emboss and letterpress, Bayt Al Qahwa
  uses gold foil.

## 3. Letterhead layout

- Logo and address in the header or footer, using the style's grid.
- Leave a clear text area for typing in Word; test with a real two-page letter.
- Provide a Word template with styles set (body, headings) and the brand font
  or an approved fallback.
- For bilingual brands, decide whether the letterhead is bilingual or
  whether there are separate Arabic (RTL) and English versions.

## 4. Email signature

- Name, role, company, one phone or email, website; optional small logo.
- Live text, not a single image (images are often blocked and are not readable
  by screen readers).
- Logo image under 30KB, hosted, with alt text.
- No inspirational quotes, no more than one social link row.

## Checklist

- [ ] Correct size and bleed for the market
- [ ] CMYK/spot, 300 dpi, vectors, crop marks
- [ ] Paper and finishes specified
- [ ] Word letterhead template tested with a real letter
- [ ] Live-text email signature
