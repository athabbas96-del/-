# Content systems

A content system makes a brand recognisable across hundreds of posts made by
different people.

## 1. Content pillars

Define three to five pillars: recurring themes the brand posts about, each
with its own template and a target share of the calendar. Example for the
fictional brand Clearpoint Clinics (Clinical Clarity):

| Pillar | Share | Template | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Health tips | 40% | Carousel, one tip per slide | "3 signs it's time for a check-up" |
| Meet the doctor | 25% | Portrait + quote | Dr. Sara Al-Amin on heart health |
| Clinic news | 20% | Announcement post + story | Same-day appointments now open |
| Awareness days | 15% | Shared template with Sunrise highlight | World Heart Day |

## 2. Grid planning

- Plan nine posts at a time and preview them as the profile grid.
- Alternate pillars and visual weights (image, type, image) so the grid has
  rhythm. Playful Pop rotates background colours; Quiet Luxury alternates
  product stills with texture close-ups.
- Keep a consistent cover treatment for Reels.

## 3. Templates and ownership

- One template per pillar and format, in the shared design library.
- A one-page "how to post" guide: fonts, colours, safe zones, caption rules,
  hashtags, tagging.
- Name the owner of each pillar and the approver for each post type.

## 4. Approvals and compliance

- Regulated sectors (health, finance, pharma) need claims checked before
  publishing. Build the review step into the calendar.
- Keep a log of licences for every image and music track used.
- Label paid partnerships and ads as the platform and local law require.

## 5. Measuring

Review monthly: reach, saves, shares and comments per pillar. Retire pillars
that consistently underperform; do not judge by single posts.

## Checklist

- [ ] 3–5 pillars with shares and templates
- [ ] Nine-post grid preview before publishing
- [ ] Owners, approvers and a how-to-post guide
- [ ] Compliance review and licence log for regulated clients
