# Social media formats

Canvas sizes used by the social examples in every style.

| Format | Size (px) | Ratio | Safe zone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram / Facebook feed post | 1080 × 1080 | 1:1 | 60px margin all round |
| Instagram feed portrait / ad | 1080 × 1350 | 4:5 | 60px margin; grid crops to 1080 × 1350 center |
| Stories and Reels | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Keep text out of top 250px and bottom 340px |
| TikTok | 1080 × 1920 | 9:16 | Keep text out of bottom 400px and right 140px |
| LinkedIn post | 1200 × 1500 or 1080 × 1080 | 4:5 / 1:1 | 60px margin |
| X / Twitter image | 1600 × 900 | 16:9 | Center 1200 × 675 for previews |
| YouTube thumbnail | 1280 × 720 | 16:9 | Keep bottom-right 200 × 60 clear (timestamp) |

Platform specs change. Check each platform's current guidance before final export.
