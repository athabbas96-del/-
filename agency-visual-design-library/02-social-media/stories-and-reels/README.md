# Stories and Reels

## 1. Safe zones (1080 × 1920)

- Top 250px: profile name and progress bar cover this area.
- Bottom 340px: reply bar, captions and buttons (TikTok needs more: about 400px
  at the bottom and 140px on the right).
- Keep headlines and CTAs in the middle area. Put backgrounds and imagery
  full-bleed.

## 2. Stills (stories)

- One headline, one action. Use the platform's link sticker for the action and
  design a visual cue toward it.
- Text over photos needs a scrim; the library's story frames use a gradient to
  about 70% black under the copy.
- Polls, questions and countdowns are native stickers: leave room for them
  rather than faking them in the artwork.

## 3. Video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts)

| Beat | Time | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 0–2s | The most striking image or line; no slow logo intro |
| Brand | by 3s | Logo, product or brand colour visible |
| Body | 3–15s | One idea, cut to the beat |
| End | last 2s | Logo and one call to action |

- Design for sound off: captions always.
- Follow the style's motion signature ([Motion](../../00-foundations/motion/README.md)):
  Kinetic Sport cuts on the beat; Quiet Luxury uses slow zooms; Playful Pop
  bounces with spring easing.
- Export 1080 × 1920, H.264 MP4, 30fps.

## 4. Covers

Reels show a cover in the profile grid. Design covers as a set (same type
position and treatment) so the grid stays tidy, and keep the cover title in
the centre 3:4 area.

## Checklist

- [ ] Text inside safe zones for every platform used
- [ ] Hook in 2 seconds, brand by 3 seconds
- [ ] Captions on; scrim under text over video
- [ ] Cover designed for the grid crop
