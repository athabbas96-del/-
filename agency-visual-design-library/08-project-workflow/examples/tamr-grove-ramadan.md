## Creative direction

Apply Modern Heritage (tradition, drawn with a modern hand) to Tamr Grove's advertising campaign for Families and corporate buyers in Riyadh and Baghdad choosing premium date gift boxes for Ramadan and Eid. Modern Heritage reworks Arab and Islamic visual culture, including geometric star patterns, arches, Kufi lettering and a palette taken from tilework and spice markets, using clean modern layouts. It is bilingual by design, with Arabic as a lead language rather than a translation. The client's existing logo, colours and typefaces stay as they are; the style governs composition, imagery, hierarchy and supporting graphic elements.

Mood: Cultural, Warm, Premium. Keywords: Rooted, Generous, Crafted, Proud, Hospitable.

**Principles for this project**

- Use arch-shaped frames (mihrab) for hero images.
- Put a geometric pattern band or border at section edges.
- Symmetry is welcome: centred titles flanked by ornaments.
- Design Arabic first
- Use patterns as structure, not wallpaper

Fit check: Food & beverage and advertising are both listed for Modern Heritage.

## Imagery

Warm, rich interiors and rituals of hospitality with deep shadows.

- Rituals: pouring coffee, serving dates, hands and vessels.
- Warm tungsten or lantern light with deep shadows.
- Textures: brass, carved wood, tile, woven textiles.
- Architecture: arches, courtyards, mashrabiya shadows.
- People shown with dignity. Respect cultural context.
