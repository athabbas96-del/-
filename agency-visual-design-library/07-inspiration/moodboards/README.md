# Moodboard briefs

A moodboard sells a direction before any design exists. Build one per style
route in a pitch. Use 9–15 images: enough to show a world, few enough to read.

**Licensing:** moodboards for internal use and pitch presentations often use
found images. Keep a source link for every image, label boards "for reference
only", and never publish or hand over a moodboard as a deliverable without
licensing the images. No moodboard images are committed to this repository for
that reason.

## Structure of a board

1. Three to five photography references that match the style's direction
2. Two or three typography specimens (the style's actual faces)
3. The palette as swatches with proportions
4. Two or three textures, materials or graphic devices
5. One or two applications (a shopfront, a package, a screen) to show it in use
