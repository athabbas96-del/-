Agency Visual Design Library

Ten ready-to-pitch visual directions for branding, social media, websites, advertising and client presentations. Each style has a full specification and worked examples, with tokens you can copy straight into a project.

  1. Match the briefFilter by the client's industry, the mood they want and where the work will run.
  2. Present the directionOpen a style and use its cover, palette and examples as a moodboard in the pitch.
  3. Adapt, then buildCopy the tokens, swap in the client's logo and colours, and keep the rules and proportions.

Sample work only. Every brand, person, address and number in the examples is invented to demonstrate the styles. None of it is client work. Photo frames are art-direction placeholders: their colours show the target grade and their captions are the shot brief.

Written guidance lives next to this page in the repository: 00-foundations/ (colour, type, grid, accessibility, Arabic and RTL) and the discipline folders 01-branding/ to 05-presentations/.

Source of truth: library/styles-data.js. The Markdown specs and tokens in visual-styles/ are generated from it.
